# Visual Style

Create a premium, restrained technology documentary, NOT a fictional melodrama.
Real Vietnamese traffic and real engineering work carry the emotion. The film
should feel attentive, precise, human and credible to an enterprise engineering team.

## Format and image

- Timeline 16:9, 1920×1080, 30 fps; target duration 170 seconds.
- Do not stretch footage. Preserve aspect ratio with contain/letterbox where needed.
- Some supplied footage is 960px wide; scaling to a 1080p timeline does not create
  new detail. Do not use generative enhancement that changes road users, signs or UI.
- Recorded inference replay is 12 fps. Do not frame-interpolate it to pretend
  the model ran faster. Natural shot cuts can hide neither false detections nor latency.
- Color: navy #071E30, white #FFFFFF, blue #0865EB; cyan #72DAFA as a restrained accent.
- Preserve exposure and signal colors in evidence footage. No dramatic recoloring
  of traffic lights, sign borders or hazard levels.

## Camera and editing

- Dashcam shots stay in the natural forward view; no synthetic drone/orbit cameras.
- Real developer: fixed over-shoulder 30–45° angle, medium shot, daylight from side.
- Real presenter: eye-level medium close-up, tripod, clean background, natural delivery.
- Clean cuts and 6–10-frame dissolves; no glitch, spinning screens or fast strobe.
- Still screenshots: slow 100→104% push-in at most, only when text remains readable.
- Show original full UI before cropping into a detail. Never insert invented UI values.
- Opening shots 3–5 seconds. Technical demonstrations 5–8 seconds or longer.
- If start-up waiting is omitted, show “Đã rút gọn thời gian khởi động”.

## Typography

- Clear Vietnamese sans-serif, for example an installed font with full diacritics.
- Use native editable text layers for titles, metrics and diagrams; not generated text in images.
- Titles about 56–72px at 1080p; subtitles 38–44px; evidence footnotes at least 26px.
- Subtitles maximum two lines, split at natural phrases. Never cover hazard banners,
  signs, event timestamps or the object being discussed. Shift subtitles up when necessary.
- Keep important content inside about 5% frame margins; preview on a phone.
- AI subtitle recognition must be manually corrected for RoadWatch, trái/phải,
  tốc độ, ki-lô-mét, AAOS, baseline and model numbers.

## Voice and music

- Narrator: natural Vietnamese, medium register, warm and controlled, not theatrical.
- Speak meaningfully, not faster to fit an overlong script. Record scene by scene.
- Opening curious; explanation clear; limitations calm; final invitation confident.
- Product voice: supplied Piper WAVs only, original timing, one at a time.
- No narrator over product voice. Lower music during both; no clipping or abrupt loudness jumps.
- Instrumental music without lyrics, restrained pulse, no horror riser or cinematic explosion.
- Music/license must cover intended commercial/presentation use; a track being visible
  in an editor is not a guarantee of permission for every distribution platform.
- If no verified track is available, use voice and clean silence. Do not fetch random music.

## Absolute exclusions

- No self-driving/braking/steering demonstration; no hands-off driving.
- No fake VinFast partnership, logo endorsement, OEM camera or CAN access.
- No synthesized team members, cloned presenter voice or invented customer testimony.
- No fake dense-mode UI, improved detections, accident avoidance or synchronized alerts.
- No generic “99% accuracy”, “zero latency”, “production-ready” or “certified safe”.
- No gore or staged crashes. The tension comes from the driver's limited attention.

Allowed generated material: neutral title backgrounds and labelled explanatory
diagrams. They are editorial illustrations, never evidence of deployed capability.
